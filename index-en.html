ABOUT

About my work

My research began with nonparametric statistics, inverse problems and quantum homodyne tomography. It now spans contrastive conditional-density estimation, causal and counterfactual inference, high-dimensional clustering, neural-network generalization and robust decentralized learning. My developing research on adversarial machine learning investigates attacks, defenses and guarantees. I combine mathematical theory, algorithm design, doctoral supervision, teaching and collaborations with academic and industrial partners.
